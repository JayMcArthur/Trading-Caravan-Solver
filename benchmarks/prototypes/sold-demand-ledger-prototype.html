Prototype A: demand ledger

Question: After Sell, can one action path carry only its growing historical-food requirement and gold-operation ledger, querying the old basket once only when concrete money is required?

This model relies on a proof obligation: for one fixed action history before the next Buy, more historical food cannot produce more current gold. Therefore the minimum survivable quantity is the only quantity that path needs.
