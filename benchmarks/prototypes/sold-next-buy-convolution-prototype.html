Prototype C: next-Buy convolution

Question: At the next Buy, can a sold food/gold curve combine with the new food price without expanding every old-food origin for every new total-food requirement?

For required total food T, split it into old food k and new food T-k. The exact winner maximizes oldGold(k) + newPrice×k. A prefix maximum compiles that choice once, turning the quadratic origin join into a linear curve build with an attached historical witness.
