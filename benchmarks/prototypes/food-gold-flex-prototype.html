THROWAWAY LOGIC PROTOTYPE

Can one food/gold flex state replace many food-Buy states?

This demo keeps every still-feasible food amount from one earlier Buy inside a single symbolic state. At each night, variants without enough food disappear; the smallest survivor becomes the amount now committed to food, and its original Buy cost is the flex gold used. Crew purchases and Interest update every surviving variant exactly. At Sell, the next Buy, or the finish, the state resolves the old Buy to its smallest surviving food amount.
