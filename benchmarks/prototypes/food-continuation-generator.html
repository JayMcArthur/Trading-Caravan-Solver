Which food buys actually enable new continuations?

This demo tests one idea: generate food quantities from the short action sequences they make survivable, instead of sweeping every integer between “minimum” and “maximum.” Counts between two thresholds enable no new continuation and are candidates to remove later. This is food-feasibility only; gold, carrying capacity, and route value still belong to the production solver.
